Consistent resource ordering eliminates circular wait because it imposes a strict linear order in which processes can request resources. By ensuring all processes acquire lock1 before lock2, it's impossible for Process A to hold lock1 and wait for lock2 while Process B simultaneously holds lock2 and waits for lock1. If Process A has lock1, Process B cannot acquire it and will block at lock1, leaving lock2 free for Process A to acquire and eventually release, thus breaking the deadlock cycle condition (Circular Wait).
