Strategy used: Resource Ordering (Deadlock Prevention).
Instead of implementing Banker's Algorithm (which requires tracking Max and Need and is computationally heavier), I assigned a global linear order to the shared resources: Printer (0) < Scanner (1) < Disk (2). Every process must request its required resources in this strict global order. This guarantees that circular wait cannot occur. No process can hold a higher-numbered resource while waiting for a lower-numbered one. Thus, deadlock is structurally prevented and multiple cycles of the program complete without hanging.
